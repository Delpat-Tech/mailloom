# Mailloom mark: explorations

The source is the Delpat logo (`cutroom/films/demos/brand/delpatlogo.png`): a navy slanted slab, a blue bowl, a navy P counter and a lower piece, with white gaps. The colours are navy #28306E and blue #3F7CBD.

Every round is kept here so the choices can be compared later.

## Round 1: clean-up (rejected)
`round1-cleanup/`. The same mark redrawn on one centre, with one gap width and one corner radius. Om: too close, "didn't change anything".
Branch `builder-b` at 45470f6 has it built with intro, outro and loader motion.

## Round 2: D and P as letters (rejected)
`round2-letters/`. Six concepts that make the D and P letters explicit (P inside D, carved P, interlock and others).
Om: drifted away from the logo. Branch `builder-b` at da733fb has "P inside D" built with motion.

## Round 3: evolutions of the original (shortlist)
`round3-evolutions/`. Eight versions, each changing one design property. `variations.png` is the board;
`top3-tiles.png` shows the top three as app icons. There is a `-icon.svg` (on its tile) and a `-mark.svg` (bare) for each.
`generate.py` builds them.

| No. | Name | What changes | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Poise | heavier slab, compact D, bold channels | safe; change is felt more than seen |
| 2 | Aligned | split runs parallel to the slant | bowl becomes a pie slice |
| 3 | Hairline | hair-thin gaps, white on navy tile | navy slab, the anchor, is lost |
| 4 | Unified | counter and lower piece merged | weakest; loses the P counter |
| 5 | Dusk | quiet gradient on each piece, white tile | closest to "our logo, but nicer" |
| 6 | Fold | slant becomes a folded facet, soft shadow | paper feel; facet may read as a new shape |
| 7 | Flap | bowl corner cut as an envelope flap | reads only once told; weakens the D |
| 8 | Aurora | white mark on navy-to-blue gradient tile | most app-like; stands out on any home screen |

Suggested split (not yet decided by Om): Aurora as the home-screen app icon, and Dusk as the mark inside the app and on white surfaces. They are the same drawing, so they read as one brand.
